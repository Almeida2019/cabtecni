## Current company description

CABTECNI, Lda is an Angolan worldwide sourcing company focused on customer satisfaction. Its services cover engineering procurement, ordering, monitoring and delivery. The company presents itself as cost-effective and dedicated, with an emphasis on a smooth process and safe arrival of goods within the agreed delivery period.

The procurement and service team is committed to best practices and a cost-effective approach to meeting and exceeding internal and external customer expectations.

NAS GLOBAL (Pty) Ltd has operated in markets including oil and gas, mining, construction and power generation. CABTECNI is based in Luanda and works with multiple OEM brands, enabling it to respond to varied client requirements.
